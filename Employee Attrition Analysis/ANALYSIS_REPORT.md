**Report Date:** July 2026

## Business Problem

The organization needs to reduce avoidable employee turnover by understanding which departments, roles, and employee segments are most at risk of leaving.

---

## Business Questions

- Which departments have the highest attrition?
- Which employee groups are most likely to leave?
- How do tenure, performance, and experience relate to attrition?
- Are there patterns by gender, age, or job role?
- What retention actions should HR prioritize?

## Key Insights

- Attrition is usually concentrated in specific departments or job groups rather than evenly spread.
- Early-tenure employees often require stronger onboarding and support.
- Workforce characteristics such as role, experience, and demographics help explain risk patterns.
- Targeted interventions are likely to be more effective than broad, generic HR actions.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Focus retention programs on the departments and roles with the highest attrition.
- Strengthen onboarding and first-year engagement for new hires.
- Review workload, growth opportunities, and manager support in high-risk groups.
- Track attrition trends regularly so HR can intervene earlier.
