# Employee Attrition Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes employee attrition patterns to help HR understand why employees leave, which groups are at higher risk, and what actions may improve retention. The goal is to turn workforce data into practical retention decisions.

---

### Business Problem
The company needs to reduce avoidable attrition by identifying departments, roles, and employee segments that show stronger turnover risk and weaker retention patterns.

### Business Questions
- Which departments have the highest attrition?
- Which employee groups are most likely to leave?
- How do tenure, performance, and work experience relate to attrition?
- Are there patterns by gender, age, or job role?
- What retention actions should HR prioritize?

### Dataset Information
- Employee records with attrition and workforce attributes
- Department, role, and demographic fields for segmentation
- Tenure and performance-related measures
- Data suitable for HR performance and retention analysis

### Data Cleaning
- Checked for missing, duplicate, and inconsistent records
- Standardized categorical values for department and role analysis
- Validated attrition-related fields and tenure values
- Prepared the data for Power BI visualization

### Exploratory Analysis
- Compared attrition by department and job role
- Reviewed retention patterns by tenure and experience
- Examined demographic differences in turnover risk
- Analyzed whether performance or satisfaction patterns align with attrition
- Looked for segments with unusually high or low retention

### Dashboard
- KPI cards for attrition rate, average tenure, and employee count
- Department and role comparison charts
- Trend views for retention and turnover patterns
- Filters for gender, department, and job segment
- Drill-down views for identifying at-risk groups

### Key Insights
- Attrition is usually concentrated in specific departments or job groups rather than evenly spread.
- Early-tenure employees often require stronger onboarding and support.
- Workforce characteristics such as role, experience, and demographics can help explain risk patterns.
- A few targeted interventions can have more impact than broad, generic HR actions.

### Business Recommendations
- Focus retention programs on the departments and roles with the highest attrition.
- Strengthen onboarding and first-year engagement for new hires.
- Review workload, growth opportunities, and manager support in high-risk groups.
- Track attrition trends regularly so HR can intervene earlier.
