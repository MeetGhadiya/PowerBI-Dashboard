# Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes sales performance across products, regions, and time periods to identify revenue drivers, profitability gaps, and target achievement trends. The goal is to help stakeholders understand what is working, where performance is leaking, and which actions can improve business outcomes.

---

### Business Problem
The business needs a clear view of sales performance so it can identify the products, regions, and time periods that contribute most to revenue and profit while also spotting underperforming areas that require intervention.

### Business Questions
- Which product categories generate the most revenue and profit?
- Which regions are outperforming or underperforming against targets?
- How do sales trends change across months or quarters?
- Where is discounting affecting profitability?
- Which areas should receive more commercial focus?

### Dataset Information
- Sales transactions with revenue, profit, and target-related fields
- Product-level and region-level performance attributes
- Time-based data for monthly or quarterly trend analysis
- Supporting fields for segmentation, filtering, and comparison

### Data Cleaning
- Removed duplicates and checked for missing or inconsistent values
- Standardized field names and data types for analysis
- Validated revenue, profit, and target metrics for accuracy
- Prepared the dataset for Power BI reporting and visualization

### Exploratory Analysis
- Analyzed revenue contribution by category and product
- Compared regional sales and profitability performance
- Reviewed target achievement patterns across segments
- Examined monthly trends to identify seasonality and momentum
- Assessed the relationship between discounts and profit margins

### Dashboard
- KPI cards for revenue, profit, target achievement, and average deal value
- Bar and column charts for category and regional comparisons
- Line charts for trend analysis over time
- Interactive slicers for product, region, and period filtering
- Drill-down views for deeper performance investigation

### Key Insights
- A small number of products and regions drive a disproportionate share of performance.
- Some segments may deliver strong revenue but weaker profit margins because of discounting or cost pressure.
- Sales performance varies over time, indicating seasonal patterns and potential pipeline fluctuations.
- Target gaps highlight where commercial attention is needed most.

### Business Recommendations
- Prioritize marketing and sales support for the highest-performing categories and regions.
- Review discounting policies in low-margin segments to protect profitability.
- Reallocate effort toward underperforming areas with strong market potential.
- Use monthly trend analysis to plan inventory, campaigns, and staffing more effectively.
- Track target performance regularly to shorten the response time for problem areas.
