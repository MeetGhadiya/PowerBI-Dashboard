# Sales Performance Analysis - Executive Report

**Report Date:** July 2026  
**Analysis Type:** Sales & Revenue Analytics  
**Prepared By:** Data Analytics Team  

---

## Business Problem

The business needs a clear view of sales performance across products, regions, and time periods to identify the main drivers of revenue and profit, as well as the areas where performance is below target.

---

## Business Questions

- Which product categories generate the most revenue and profit?
- Which regions are performing best against sales targets?
- How do revenue and profitability change over time?
- Where is discounting affecting margin performance?
- Which segments require immediate commercial attention?

---

## Dataset Information

- Sales transactions with revenue, profit, and target-related fields
- Product-level and region-level attributes for segmentation
- Time-based fields for monthly or quarterly trend analysis
- Supporting dimensions for filtering, comparison, and drill-down

---

## Data Cleaning

- Removed duplicate records and checked for missing values
- Standardized field names and data types for reporting
- Validated revenue, profit, and target measures
- Prepared the dataset for Power BI dashboarding

## Exploratory Analysis

### Revenue Performance by Product Category
- Identified the product categories contributing most to sales
- Compared high-revenue and low-revenue segments
- Reviewed category mix for portfolio balance

### Regional Performance Analysis
- Compared revenue distribution across regions
- Evaluated profit margins by geography
- Highlighted regions with stronger and weaker target performance

### Target Achievement Analysis
- Measured actual sales against target values
- Identified gaps where performance falls short
- Compared target achievement across products and regions

### Profitability Insights
- Reviewed how discounting affects profit
- Compared high-volume and high-margin segments
- Assessed where cost pressure may be reducing returns

### Time-Based Trends
- Studied monthly and quarterly performance patterns
- Identified seasonality and momentum shifts
- Observed changes in sales consistency over time

---

## Dashboard

- KPI cards for revenue, profit, target achievement, and average deal value
- Trend charts to track performance across time
- Bar and column charts for category and regional comparisons
- Interactive filters for product, region, and time period
- Drill-down views for deeper analysis at segment level

---

## Key Insights

- A small set of products and regions contribute most of the overall performance.
- Some segments generate strong revenue but weaker margins because of discounting or cost pressure.
- Target gaps show where sales teams and business leaders should focus attention.
- Time-based patterns suggest that performance is influenced by seasonality and pipeline movement.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize commercial investment in the strongest-performing categories and regions.
- Review discount policies in low-margin segments to protect profitability.
- Reallocate sales effort to underperforming areas with growth potential.
- Use monthly trend monitoring to support planning for inventory, campaigns, and staffing.
- Track target performance consistently so corrective action happens sooner.
