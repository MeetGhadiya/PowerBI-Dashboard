## Business Problem

The retail business needs to identify which products, regions, and customer segments drive profit while also spotting where discounts, shipping, or category mix reduce performance.

---

## Business Questions

- Which categories and sub-categories generate the most sales and profit?
- Which regions or segments perform best?
- How do discounts affect profitability?
- Are there shipping or fulfillment patterns that hurt performance?
- Which customer segments should receive more attention?

---

- Shipping-related attributes for operational analysis
- Data suited for sales and margin trend analysis

## Exploratory Analysis

### Category and Sub-Category Performance
- Compared sales and profit across categories
- Identified high-volume and high-margin combinations
- Reviewed where profit is weaker than sales volume suggests

### Regional and Segment Analysis
- Reviewed regional performance and segment mix
- Compared customer groups across the retail business
- Highlighted segments with stronger and weaker profitability

### Discount Impact
- Assessed the effect of discounts on margin outcomes
- Looked for discount-heavy areas that reduce profit
- Evaluated where pricing discipline matters most

### Shipping and Operational Patterns
- Examined shipping performance and delivery patterns
- Investigated whether operational factors affect business outcomes
- Reviewed areas where service issues may hurt efficiency

---

## Key Insights

- High sales does not always mean high profit.
- Discounts can materially reduce margin if overused.
- Performance varies across regions and customer segments.
- Operational factors such as shipping can influence overall satisfaction and efficiency.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritize categories and segments that consistently produce profit.
- Review discounting strategy in low-margin areas.
- Investigate shipping issues that affect customer experience or cost.
- Use regional and segment analysis to improve inventory and sales focus.
