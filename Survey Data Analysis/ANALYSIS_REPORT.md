# Survey Data Analysis - Executive Report

**Report Date:** July 2026  
**Analysis Type:** Survey and Feedback Analytics  
**Prepared By:** Data Analytics Team  

---

## Business Problem

The organization needs to understand how employees or respondents feel about their experience, where satisfaction is strongest or weakest, and which areas need improvement.

---

## Business Questions

- How satisfied are respondents overall?
- Which departments or groups report stronger or weaker sentiment?
- What is the recommendation likelihood?
- Are there patterns by gender, role, or time period?
- Which areas should leadership address first?

---

## Dataset Information

- Survey responses from 200+ respondents
- Satisfaction and recommendation-related measures
- Department and demographic fields for segmentation
- Data suitable for sentiment and experience analysis

---

## Data Cleaning

- Checked for incomplete or inconsistent survey responses
- Standardized rating scales and categorical fields
- Validated respondent segmentation values
- Prepared the data for Power BI visualization

---

## Exploratory Analysis

### Satisfaction Analysis
- Compared satisfaction across departments and groups
- Identified where feedback is strongest and weakest
- Reviewed overall sentiment distribution

### Recommendation Likelihood
- Reviewed recommendation rates across respondent segments
- Compared recommendation behavior with satisfaction scores
- Highlighted groups with stronger engagement

### Segment Comparison
- Examined whether demographic patterns affect feedback
- Compared outcomes by department and respondent group
- Looked for specific areas with lower scores

### Time-Based Feedback Trends
- Looked for changes in responses over time
- Assessed whether sentiment is improving or declining
- Identified periods that may need leadership attention

---

## Dashboard

- KPI cards for satisfaction and recommendation rate
- Department comparison charts
- Demographic and group-level sentiment visuals
- Filters for department, gender, and time period
- Drill-down views for deeper feedback analysis

---

## Key Insights

- Satisfaction is often uneven across departments.
- Recommendation rates can highlight stronger engagement than satisfaction alone.
- A few specific groups may consistently report lower scores.
- Time-based tracking helps reveal whether experience is improving or declining.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Address the departments with the weakest feedback first.
- Use follow-up surveys to validate the root causes of dissatisfaction.
- Track satisfaction and recommendation metrics regularly.
- Compare strong and weak groups to identify practices worth repeating.
