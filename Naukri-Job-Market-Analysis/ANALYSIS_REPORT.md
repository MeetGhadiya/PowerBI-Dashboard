# Naukri Job Market Analysis - Executive Report

**Report Date:** July 2026  
**Analysis Type:** Job Market and Hiring Analytics  
**Prepared By:** Data Analytics Team  

---

## Business Problem

The job market is large and fragmented, so stakeholders need a clear view of which roles, cities, skills, and experience levels are in highest demand and where salary opportunities are strongest.

---

## Business Questions

- Which job categories and roles have the highest demand?
- Which cities have the most job opportunities?
- What experience levels are most frequently requested?
- Which skills appear most often across postings?
- How do salary levels vary by market segment?

---

## Dataset Information

- Job postings collected from Naukri.com
- Role, company, city, salary, and experience fields
- Category-level labels for market segmentation
- Data suitable for demand and salary analysis

---

## Data Cleaning

- Standardized job titles, skills, and city names
- Removed duplicates and corrected inconsistent records
- Parsed salary and experience fields for analysis
- Prepared the dataset for Power BI reporting

## Exploratory Analysis

### Demand by Category and Role
- Compared job counts across categories and roles
- Identified high-demand job families
- Reviewed recruiter concentration across the market

### City and Geography Analysis
- Analyzed city-wise job demand and salary patterns
- Highlighted the most active hiring locations
- Compared opportunities across major job hubs

### Experience Requirements
- Reviewed experience levels requested in job postings
- Identified the most common experience bands
- Checked whether demand changes by job category

### Skills and Compensation
- Identified the most common technical and functional skills
- Compared salary levels across roles and cities

## Dashboard

---

## Key Insights

- Job demand is concentrated in a few major cities and recruiters.
- Mid-level experience is often the most requested in the market.
- Core technical skills such as SQL, Python, and Java appear repeatedly.
- Salary varies significantly by role, city, and experience band.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Job seekers should align skill development with the most common market requirements.
- Recruiters should benchmark salary and role demand against city-level competition.
- Training programs should focus on the experience bands and skills most in demand.
- Market watchers should track shifts in top recruiters and growing job categories.
